# Barber

Barber is a free, simple appointment diary for mobile barbers, for Android. Visits out, clients at home and venues sit in one diary, with no menu to set up, several people on one visit, a Due for a trim list, the travel between visits worked out, out days and home days, the day's route in Maps, mileage at HMRC's rates, call-out fees, client notes and photos, takings with a PDF report, widgets and password-locked backups. It has no adverts, no accounts and no paid version, and it cannot go online.
