The diary for barbers on the move.

Barber is a simple appointment diary for Android for a mobile barber. No menus to build: a booking is a time, a price and any notes. It works out the travel between visits, books a dad and his lads as one visit and tells you which regulars are due a trim.

It is free, with no subscription, no fees on new clients and no adverts. Everything stays on your phone, encrypted, and the app cannot go online.

How it works

Setting up takes a couple of minutes: your hours, your services and your prices, with a ready-made start you can change.

  1. Book

    Tap +, pick the client, the time and where. Barber fills in your usual cut, the travel and the call-out. Add more heads for a family.

  2. Drive

    The day's visits in order, opened in Maps in one tap.

  3. Get paid

    Tap Take payment when you finish, rebook in one tap, and see the takings and miles add up.

Installing and updating

Barber is not on Google Play, so your phone asks a couple of extra questions the first time.

  1. Download the app file

    Tap Download on your phone. The file is called Barber.apk and is about 2.4 MB. If Chrome says the file might be harmful, choose Download anyway.

  2. Allow the install

    Open the file. The first time, Android asks you to allow your browser to install apps. Allow it, go back and tap Install. If Google Play Protect steps in, choose More details, then Install anyway.

  3. Updating later

    Barber tells you when a newer version is out. Install the new file over the old one and everything you have saved is kept.

Questions

Who is Barber for?

A barber working on their own who visits clients at home, in care homes or at weddings, and may also cut at home. It keeps one diary for one person.

Is Barber really free?

Yes. There is no paid version, no subscription, no fee on new clients and no adverts. Nothing in the app is locked.

Is it on Google Play?

No. Barber is a personal project and is downloaded from this site as an Android app file. It needs Android 8 or later, which covers almost every phone in use.

Do I have to set up a price list?

No. Tell Barber how long your usual cut takes and what you charge, once. Every new booking starts from that, and a regular starts from what they had last time. Change the length or price on any booking.

Can I book a dad and his kids together?

Yes. On the booking, tap Add another person. Everyone is cut back to back at the same address, with one journey and one call-out, and each person has their own length and price.

How does the Due list work?

Once a client has had a few cuts, Barber works out their usual gap. When it is up, or nearly, and they have nothing booked, they show on the Due list. Text opens your messages with a ready-written note you can change before sending. Nothing is sent by itself.

How does it know how far away a client is?

You tell it, once, on the client card: how many minutes from your home and how many miles. Barber does not look anything up online, so it works with no signal and nobody tracks where you go. The drive between two clients is a guess until you set it, and then it is remembered.

Is the mileage figure right for my tax return?

It adds up the miles you put on client cards and venues, and works out the claim at HMRC's simplified mileage rates for the self-employed: 55p a mile for the first 10,000 business miles in a tax year from 6 April 2026, and 25p after. Rates can change, so check GOV.UK each April and change them in Settings. It is a guide, not tax advice: check with your accountant.

Does it need the internet?

No. The app does not ask Android for internet access at all, so it cannot go online. The route and directions open in your maps app, texts go out through your own phone, and WhatsApp messages open in WhatsApp for you to send.

Where are my clients' details kept?

Only on your phone, in the app's private storage, encrypted with a key held by Android. Nothing is uploaded, and the developer receives nothing.

Can my clients book online?

No. Barber is your own diary, kept on your phone. Clients book with you by phone, message or in person.

What about GDPR?

Client details, addresses included, stay on your phone and are never shared. Barber can give a client a copy of their record, forget a client completely, find clients you no longer need to keep, and includes a privacy notice you can show clients.

What if I forget my PIN?

There is no way round the PIN, not even for the developer. You can clear the app's storage in your phone's settings, which deletes everything, and then restore your latest backup.

Who made it?

Barber is made by Jonathan Jackson, an independent developer in the United Kingdom. It is not connected with any other booking software company.
