Release notes

What changed in each version of Barber. The newest is at the top.

Version 1.0.0 21 November 2026Latest

  • First release. One diary for one mobile barber, with every booking at the client's home, at your home or at a venue, each in its own colour.
  • No menu to set up: a booking is a time, a length, a price and optional notes, starting from your usual cut. Regulars start from what they had last time.
  • Several people on one visit, each with their own length and price, with one journey and one call-out.
  • A Due for a trim list worked out from each regular's usual gap, with a ready-written text you can change in Settings, and a banner in the diary when regulars are due.
  • Travel linked up across the day: from home to the first visit, from visit to visit, and home after the last, blocked out in the diary and counted in clash checks.
  • Client cards with address, minutes from home and miles, notes, an alert and photos. Journeys between two clients remembered once set, and marked as a guess until then.
  • Working days set as out, at home or both, with one-off changes. Free times and the next free slot only offer what fits.
  • Venues such as care homes and weddings, remembered, with no travel between bookings at the same place.
  • The day's route: every stop in order with the drive between, opened in Google Maps from home and back, and a text of your day for someone you trust.
  • Call-out fees added to visits, changeable on any booking, and shown in takings.
  • Mileage for any period with the claim at HMRC's simplified rates (55p then 25p a mile from April 2026), in the Takings tab, the PDF report and the spreadsheets.
  • Two reminder wordings: one for visits, and one for clients coming to you with your address and parking note.
  • Day, week and list views, drag and stretch, time off and UK bank holidays, repeat bookings, takings and reports, widgets, the phone calendar copy, imports, data protection tools, password-locked backups and the app lock.

Checking your download

The file for version 1.0.0 is called Barber.apk. It is 2.4 MB (2,536,794 bytes). Its SHA-256 checksum is:

c64505e3cc971ab1644eea64dc7c90ccf9ff7e147bc0a8a5cab4382312e342e5

On Windows, run certutil -hashfile Barber.apk SHA256 and compare. Every version is signed with the same key, so each one installs over the last and keeps what you have saved.
