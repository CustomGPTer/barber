The first release of Barber, a free, simple appointment diary for mobile barbers on Android.

- No menu to set up: a booking is a time, a length, a price and any notes. Regulars start from what they had last time.
- Several people on one visit, such as a dad and his lads, with one journey and one call-out.
- A Due for a trim list of regulars whose usual gap is up, with a ready-written text.
- Visits out, clients at your home and venues such as care homes, in one diary, each in its own colour.
- Travel linked up across the day: from home, from visit to visit, and home again.
- Out days and home days, the day's route in Google Maps, and your day sent to someone you trust.
- Mileage with the claim at HMRC's simplified rates, and call-out fees.
- Reminders, client notes and photos, repeat bookings, takings with a PDF report, widgets and password-locked backups.
- No adverts, no account, no monthly fee. It cannot go online.

Android 8 or later. Full notes: https://customgpter.github.io/barber/releases.html
